## What changed

Every leg of the chain was wired by an earlier slice (N2077–N2083 power, N2088–N2093 pump/conduit/drill, N2108–N2120 factory ticks) but no slice had ever driven the chain end to end and asserted the session state before/after, so N2121 adds one pure read of the live session and proves it over 900 deterministic ticks (300 + 600) from a reset state.

- New `SourceSessionSnapshotState.source_session_snapshot_state(game)` reads the live game object only, in one pure pass (16 fields): the core's held copper (`ItemModule.get`, ItemModule.java:209) and health (`HealthComp.health`, HealthComp.java:11); the placed power graph grouped at one live tick (`PowerGraph.add` groups each building once, PowerGraph.java:277-301) for needed (`getPowerNeeded`, PowerGraph.java:105), produced (`getPowerProduced`, :95), battery stored (`getBatteryStored`, :118 — status × capacity over enabled batteries; the grouped dicts rebuild `power_status` as 0.0 every frame, so the live status is read from the battery sprite meta instead) and capacity (`getTotalBatteryCapacity`, :142), with coverage from `distributePower` (PowerGraph.java:189-190); the drill's `DrillBuild` fields from the legacy per-cell stores the tick reads (`timeDrilled += warmup * delta()`, Drill.java:296; `warmup` approaches `speed`, Drill.java:304; `lastDrillSpeed = (speed * dominantItems * warmup) / delay`, Drill.java:303; produced count from `offload(dominantItem)`, Drill.java:317; delay = `getDrillTime(copper)` = (600 + 50 × 1) / 1 = 650 ticks, Drill.java:170-172 with `hardnessDrillMultiplier` 50f at :28 and copper hardness 1) and its held water (`LiquidModule.get`, LiquidModule.java:102); the pump's `PumpBuild` frame (`warmup`, `totalProgress`, Pump.java:111) and held water; and the water held across the whole conduit line. A null game, or a game that has not entered the playable slice, reads all zeros.
- New owner constants `PORT_GAME_SOURCE_SESSION_SNAPSHOT_STATE` and `PORT_GAME_SOURCE_FACTORY_CONVEYOR_TICK_DELTA_STATE` in `SourceRuntimeOwnerGameplayRegistry.gd`.

## The belt bug this proof exposed

Proving the chain exposed a live-loop bug, fixed in this slice. Before the fix, in a 900-tick session no copper ever reached the core: the drill produced 6 items, the first was dumped to the first belt cell and sat there, `drill_items` grew 1..5 while `core_copper` stayed 0.

- Root cause: `simulate_factory` passed its delta in seconds straight into the belt advance, but the advance moves items by `moved = speed * edelta()` (Conveyor.java:262) and `edelta` is in ticks — `Time.delta` is the real frame delta scaled to 60fps ticks (arc Time.java:26, `* 60f`), and `BuildingComp.delta()`/`edelta()` carry it unchanged apart from `timeScale` and efficiency (BuildingComp.java:1922-1929). The belts therefore advanced 60× too slowly (0.0005/cell per tick instead of 0.03), and the first item sitting at the belt entry blocked every later dump on the 0.4 item spacing. The N2070 proof never caught it because its live-loop check calls `simulate_factory(game, 1.0)`, where 1.0 second and 1.0 tick coincide numerically.
- Fix: new `SourceFactoryRuntimeState.source_factory_conveyor_tick_delta_state(seconds)` converts at the live-loop boundary (the N2093 pump precedent) and `simulate_factory` advances the belts by ticks. The wrapper's contract is unchanged — its delta was already in ticks (the N2070 proof pins 1.0 == one tick of movement) — and the N2070 proof still passes.
- After the fix the 900-tick session delivers `core_copper` 0 → 4 (produced 6, the 5th and 6th items still in transit at tick 900), with the drill staying boosted and the pump/conduit water matching the pre-fix measurements exactly.
